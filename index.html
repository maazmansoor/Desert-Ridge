Our Philosophy

From preschool to graduation, every student, from any circumstance, can find their learning style and strengths.

Mission

Desert Ridge educates, enlightens, and empowers all students to achieve their full potential as part of an investigative, innovative, and inclusive-centered community.

Vision

Desert Ridge students will possess the passion to infuse intellectual inquiry by working with families, fellow students and the community to successfully navigate and create a positive change in the world.

Values
  • Family Engagement
  • Lifelong Learning
  • Community Involvement
  • Inclusion
